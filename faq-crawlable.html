Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to Common Questions About Direct Primary Care

Learn how membership, insurance, appointments, communication, labs, and emergency care work at Firebird Direct Primary Care.

Understanding DPC

What sets Firebird DPC apart?

Firebird DPC emphasizes direct access, predictable monthly fees, patient participation in care decisions, and more time with the physician through in-office, virtual, phone, text, and email communication.

Insurance

What if I already have insurance?

The current Firebird website recommends keeping insurance or another cost-sharing arrangement for hospitalizations, specialists, emergencies, and other major healthcare costs. DPC membership is not health insurance.

Membership

How do I become a member?

Firebird directs interested patients to complete the contact form or use the Atlas enrollment link.

Appointments

Will I have a wait time?

The practice states that it sees patients on time and offers same-day to next-day appointments during the week.

Communication

How can members communicate?

Members may use secure patient portal messaging, texting, phone, and secure telehealth appointments when appropriate.

Labs and outside services

How do labs, imaging, and referrals work?

The practice may help patients access low-cost cash-pay labs and imaging options, while insurance may still be used for outside services when appropriate.

Emergencies

Does DPC replace emergency care?

No. Direct primary care does not replace emergency care, hospitalization, surgery, specialist care, or catastrophic coverage.
