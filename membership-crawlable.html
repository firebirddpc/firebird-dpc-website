Additional children: $45/month for each child under 18 beyond the first in a member-plus-child plan or the second in a two-adult family plan.

Household eligibility: Couple and family plans include spouses or domestic partners. Member-plus-child and family rates apply regardless of the adults’ ages. Children must be under 18.

Students & trainees: The $75 monthly rate is available to individuals under 26 enrolled full time in college, graduate or professional school, vocational school, or a residency or fellowship program. Proof of full-time enrollment or training is required at enrollment and annually thereafter. Eligibility ends on the 26th birthday or when qualifying full-time enrollment or training ends.

Payment options: Quarterly prepayment equals three times the applicable monthly rate; annual prepayment equals twelve times that rate. No discount applies for longer payment periods.

Clear expectations

What Your Membership Covers—and What May Cost Extra

Included in your monthly membership

Routine primary care visits, preventive and chronic condition management, and direct communication with your physician, as described above. There is no additional copay for included visits.

Potential additional charges

Outside laboratory testing, imaging, medications, medical supplies, and certain office procedures may have separate costs. Ask the practice about pricing before arranging services.

Important: DPC membership is not health insurance and does not pay for emergency department visits, hospitalization, specialist services, or other care outside the membership.

Common questions

Membership FAQs

Do I need health insurance to join?

No. You can enroll without health insurance, although coverage for major medical expenses is recommended.

Is there an enrollment fee?

No. Firebird does not charge an enrollment fee for a new membership.

Can I use HSA funds for membership fees?

HSA eligibility depends on applicable tax rules and your individual circumstances. Check current IRS guidance or consult your tax adviser before using HSA funds.

Can my family join?

Yes. Individual, child, couple, and family options are listed above. Each additional child under 18 costs $45/month beyond the first child in a member-plus-child plan or the second child in a two-adult family plan.

How do I reach my physician between visits?

Members can use the practice's direct communication options, including secure messaging, text, and phone, when appropriate.

When does my rate change if my age or eligibility changes?

For monthly memberships, a rate change resulting from an age or other eligibility change takes effect on the first day of the following month. Quarterly and annual prepaid memberships retain their existing rate through the current prepaid term; the applicable new rate begins with the next prepaid term.
