What membership includes

Primary Care Without Per-Visit Copays

Direct access to Dr. Hagen

Ongoing primary care and direct physician communication.

Annual physical examination

Preventive care and periodic wellness planning.

Longer visits when needed

More time for symptoms, medications, chronic conditions, and health goals.

Illness and injury visits

Appointments for sudden illness or injury when primary care is appropriate.

Multiple communication options

Secure portal messaging, texting, phone, and telehealth when clinically appropriate.

Lower-cost outside services

Discounts on imaging and lab testing, with in-house blood draws available.

Selected office procedures

Low-cost or at-cost offerings for selected injections, EKG, simple dermatologic procedures, and simple wound suturing.

No copay for included visits

No per-visit copay or hidden billing for care included in the membership.

DPC is not health insurance. Firebird DPC membership covers the primary care services included in the membership. Insurance or another appropriate form of coverage is still recommended for emergencies, hospitalization, surgery, specialty care, and other major medical expenses.
