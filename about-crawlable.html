Meet Dr. Mark Hagen, DO & the Firebird DPC Team

Firebird Direct Primary Care is a physician-led family medicine practice in Worthington, Ohio, created to make primary care more personal, accessible, and transparent.

Meet the care team

Dr. Mark Hagen, DO

Osteopathic Physician · Board-Certified in Family Medicine

Dr. Mark Hagen grew up in upstate New York and earned a B.S. in Medical Technology from West Virginia University before attending the West Virginia School of Osteopathic Medicine.

He completed his Family Medicine residency at Doctors Hospital in Columbus, served as Chief Resident, and created Firebird Direct Primary Care to offer a more personal, accessible, and transparent approach to family medicine.

His care philosophy centers on knowing patients personally and creating time for thoughtful, direct primary care. Learn more about Direct Primary Care in Worthington, Ohio.
