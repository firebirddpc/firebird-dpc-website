A different primary care model

What Is Direct Primary Care?

Direct primary care, or DPC, is a membership model for primary care. At Firebird DPC, members pay a predictable membership fee for the primary care services included in their membership rather than paying a copay for each routine visit.

The model is designed to reduce barriers between patients and their primary care physician, allowing more time for prevention, follow-up, questions, and ongoing management.

Membership-based care

More Direct Access to Primary Care

Membership is designed to make it easier to reach the practice when questions or concerns arise. Depending on the situation, members can connect through office visits, secure messaging, phone, text, or telehealth when clinically appropriate.

There are no copays for the routine primary care visits included in membership, and Firebird publishes its membership pricing so patients can see the cost before enrolling.
